Scope options (enum)

single — only this surface participates (if enabled).

all — all enabled surfaces in that lane participate at once.

rotate — one surface at a time, cycling through the eligible surfaces.

random — one surface chosen randomly from eligible surfaces each prompt.

What “eligible surfaces” means
For a given lane, eligible surfaces are those that are:

enabled for that lane (Prompt/Mark/Persist), and

not explicitly disallowed by the preset or match options.

Rotation semantics (canon default)

rotate/random for Prompt Scope advances per prompt event (each new question/note generated).
